Arc 0 — The Accident 

This repository documents the origin of Full AI Works (this is what I was calling my method/story/system at the time), from first AI experiments to structured save points and triad systems, serving as both narrative and system reference

Back in July 2025, my sister downloaded ChatGPT and started showing it off.  
By “showing it off,” I mean both her and the AI roasting me.  
So I downloaded the app. Not to get even — just to see how deep the well actually went.  
I’d never used AI before. I didn’t have a plan. I just kept poking it to see what broke.  
Instead of roasting my sister back, I asked the AI to collaborate with me on an original RPG / anime-style story.  
That’s when I hit the first wall.  
Every new session the AI forgot everything.  
So I did the simplest thing I could think of:  
I asked the AI to summarize our conversation.  
I saved it.  
Copied it.  
Pasted it into the next session.

So I began getting the AI to make master sheets like these \-

Timeline: August  2025 – First Master Sheets

Overview

Arc 1 documents the initial process of creating the Yggdrasil Trilogy universe (my story)  and the workflow that would later evolve into the Codex.

The challenge: producing a coherent, immersive narrative while working non-linearly across multiple AI sessions. This arc captures the solutions developed to overcome memory limitations, timeline fragmentation, and structural consistency.

\---

Context

Discovery: First exposure to AI tools (ChatGPT) via a family demo.

Initial Experimentation: Collaborative RPG/anime-style storytelling.

Problem: Each new AI session forgot prior context, making continuity impossible.
